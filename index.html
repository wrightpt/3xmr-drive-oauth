Private operator utility · Google Drive OAuth

Secure, owner-controlled backup access.

3XMR Homelab Drive Backups is a private utility that helps its operator use rclone to store and verify selected infrastructure backup archives in Google Drive and retrieve them for authorized recovery.

What this application does

After the operator explicitly authorizes Google access, the local backup utility can use the granted Drive permissions to upload, list, verify, and download designated backup objects. The operator controls the selected accounts, storage destinations, permissions, retention, and restore procedures.

Backup archives must be encrypted client-side before this new connection is activated for production. Google Drive remains the chosen off-site storage provider. No backup archives or access tokens are served from this public website.

Limited access

Google OAuth permission is requested for backup and recovery functions only, under an explicitly authorized account.

Operator controlled

This is not a public backup service. There are no customer accounts, public backup endpoints, or self-service registrations.

Transparency

Learn how Google user data is handled, retained, and revoked in the privacy policy.

Access and support

Only the authorized operator may connect Google accounts. You can review or revoke a grant at Google Account connections. For questions about this application, contact wrightpt@gmail.com.

This static informational website does not request Google account credentials, run a sign-in form, or perform backups itself.
