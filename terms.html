The 3XMR Homelab Drive Backups application is an owner-operated utility for storing and recovering selected infrastructure backup archives via Google Drive. It is not offered as a public storage or managed backup service.

Authorized operation

Only the operator or an expressly authorized person may connect Google accounts or use the backup utility. Use requires compliance with Google's account, Drive, and OAuth terms and the permissions granted during authorization.

Informational website

This website exists to describe the application's purpose and privacy practices. It does not accept backup uploads, store OAuth secrets, offer public account creation, or expose infrastructure controls.

Backup and recovery responsibility

The operator is responsible for choosing backup data, verifying encryption and restore procedures, reviewing storage quotas, retaining recovery credentials, and managing access. No guarantee of uninterrupted access or successful restoration is made by this informational website.

Privacy and authorization

Google user data is handled as described in the Privacy policy. The account owner can revoke OAuth authorization in Google Account connections.
